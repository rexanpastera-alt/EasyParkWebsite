Easy Park transforms the WVSU-Pototan Campus commute by replacing parking uncertainty with intelligent, real-time camera tracking.

Traditional Campus Parking Delays

Students and faculty waste up to 15 minutes searching for vacant spots during morning rushes, leading to congestion at campus entry points.

  • Late arrival for morning classes & faculty duties
  • Unnecessary fuel consumption & vehicle wear
  • Lack of dedicated real-time motorcycle tracking
Impact: Gate bottlenecks & high daily commuter frustration.

The Easy Park Solution

Powered by low-cost ESP32 visual cameras that continuously scan slot occupancy, giving commuters live spot visibility on their phones before arrival.

  • Park in under 2 minutes with direct spot visibility
  • Instant zero-fee slot reservation & tracking
  • Specialized bays for Cars & Motorcycles
Impact: Seamless, stress-free, and organized campus access.

System Specifications

Engineered for Campus Efficiency

Combining low-cost micro-hardware with scalable cloud analytics.

ESP32 Camera Modules

Low-cost overhead vision sensors that eliminate expensive ground trenching while providing high slot accuracy.

Dual Vehicle Classification

Custom algorithms engineered to detect narrow motorcycle bays and standard automobile spaces simultaneously.

Instant Cloud Sync

Real-time status updates broadcast directly to mobile user apps and administrative security dashboards.

Campus Security Log

Provides security administration with hourly utilization logs, bottleneck alerts, and gate traffic reports.
